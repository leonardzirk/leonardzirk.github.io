Colony 3D
~ renders from an experiment in simulated life ~

These are renders I created in my experimental journey while learning to grow living things inside a computer (2026). A new technique being incorporated is marching‑cubes, which is great for surfaces. All pictures are made from scratch — no textures, no clay, only two nutrient gradients and one rule. I started on a laptop and now render on the GPU. Stages 1 to 3 are 100% single cells. I will be adding regularly I hope, so drop by now and then. 09/26.

There are 10 stages. Thanks and enjoy.
